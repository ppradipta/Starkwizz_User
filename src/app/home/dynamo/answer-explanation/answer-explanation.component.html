Answer Explanation Feature
Temporarily Paused

At present, the Answer Explanation feature is temporarily paused based on the recommendation of our academic experts and guidance team.

Students already receive explanations from school teachers and tuition teachers.

Since different teachers may use different solving styles, providing another explanation here may cause confusion or unnecessary comparison for students.

For now, Strakwizz focuses on helping students evaluate their preparation and identify weak areas, while teachers continue to guide them through explanations.
