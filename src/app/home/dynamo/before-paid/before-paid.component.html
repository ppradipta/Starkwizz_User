DYNAMO

Academic Methodical Test Series [5 Steps Test & Analysis]

Dynamo platform is a unique concept meant for the e-assessment and analysis of children (Standard 3-10) which would be carried out through five steps such as, individual chapter-wise practice and revision tests, multi-chapters wise quarterly and mid-term tests, and a final test comprising of all the chapters and subjects based on the updated school syllabus of CBSE and ICSE education board. Dynamo provides a 24x7 platform where the children can carry out their self-assessments irrespective of their source of education. These tests are not time bound and children can assess themselves upon completion of a chapter in the school. The purpose of the Dynamo platform is to monitor children’s learning and provide valuable feedback to help them modify their goals. This platform allows children to visually organize their thoughts, connect ideas, and gains a deeper understanding of various topics and subjects. This platform is designed and developed in such a way that the children from different Board would have a better growth rate by going through regular systematic assessments with a vision “BETTER THE ASSESSMENT, BETTER THE GROWTH”. These objective exams are not time bound and children can assess themselves on completion of each chapter, without doing any modifications or changes to their regular busy study schedule. Research shows that learning and practice objectives are helpful as they capture children’s attention, which can make them more engaged and can improve their academic performance. Intelligent server-based these MCQ tests are prepared by the top most educators for each and every chapter covered according to the updated syllabus. It is a platform which would be truly helpful for both children and parents as children would be able to assess their level of understanding on a topic by going through regular systematic assessments and parents would be able to easily monitor the progress of their child education.

DIAGNOSTIC TEST :

DIAGNOSTIC tests for all the chapters of a subject are always in the activated form. On completion of a chapter in the school, children would be able to appear the DIAGNOSTIC test on that particular chapter. It would help children to assess and analysis their preliminary understanding on that particular chapter. It would guide the children know their weak areas in a chapter and where they need to improve through real time reports.

PROGRESSIVE TEST :

After completion of the DIAGNOSTIC test, children would have an opportunity to learn and revise before appearing the same chapter again through PROGRESSIVE test. The children must have to secure 100% score to qualify the PROGRESSIVE test within a term of five (5) times maximum.

1st QUARTER TEST :

After completion of PROGRESSIVE tests for chapters belonging to 25% of a particular subject, the children would be able to appear in the 1st QUARTER test comprising of questions related to that 25% chapters only.

2nd QUARTER TEST :

For 2nd QUARTER test the children need to follow the same procedure of appearing DIAGNOSTIC and PROGRESSIVE tests for the next 25% chapters belonging to a particular subject.

1st PROFICIENCY TEST

Once a child completes the 1st and 2nd QUARTER test, the 1 ST PROFICIENCY test will be activated. This test will comprise of questions related to the first 50% completed chapters of a particular subject.

3rd QUARTER TEST

For 3rd QUARTER test the children need to follow the same procedure of appearing DIAGNOSTIC and PROGRESSIVE tests for the third 25% chapters belonging to a particular subject.

4th QUARTER TEST

For 4th QUARTER test the children need to follow the same procedure of appearing DIAGNOSTIC and PROGRESSIVE tests for the last 25% chapters belonging to a particular subject.

2nd PROFICIENCY TEST

Once a child completes the 3rd and 4th QUARTER test, the 2 ND PROFICIENCY test will be activated. This test will comprise of questions related to the last 50% completed chapters of a particular subject.

BENCHMARK TEST

The Final BENCHMARK test will be activated on completion of the 2 ND PROFICIENCY Test for a particular subject. This test would comprise of questions related to all the completed chapters for a subject.
