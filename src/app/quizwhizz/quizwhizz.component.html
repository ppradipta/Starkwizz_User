QUIZ WHIZZ

The Game Changer

Quiz Whizz platform encourages the children (Standard 3-10) to participate in the weekly “Sunday – Quiz Whizz” gaming competition program on General Knowledge, Current Affairs and Mental Ability including academic GK Syllabus. This fun, creative and interactive competition program is totally based on the children’s “recall and time management” skill. Children from CBSE and ICSE education board can regularly play and assess their IQ and memory skills by attending the Quiz Whizz program throughout the year. This platform test an aspirant’s knowledge and awareness of the world around them, encompassing topics like history, geography, science, innovations and inventions, economy, polities, world affairs, sports, logical reasoning and recent global events. This effective platform not only gives an opportunity to complete the academic GK syllabus in time, but also helps a lot to bring an improvement in their individual’s knowledge and skill. This is really helps in the holistic development of children by building skills like; problem solving, decision making, critical thinking, memory, time management, comprehensive and conversational skill. It gives the children a chance to enhance their awareness of various national and international topics and events. This platform genuinely supports children’s academic performance and lays the foundation for future challenges and lifelong learning.

COMPETITION INFO
  • Children are able to participate in the Quiz Whizz completions on every Sunday throughout the year.
  • There are two levels of Quiz Whizz competition to be participating by the children in a month. Level 1: Turtle Drive - Every SUNDAY ((except the last SUNDAY of the month)) Level 2: Horse Ride - Last SUNDAY of a month *Here in this Quiz Whizz platform, the Turtle Drive competitions are known as “Learn & Practice” and the Horse Ride competitions are referred as “Terminal” session.
  • Children can start the game in a month through Turtle Drive competitions and accelerate to win the game at the end of the month through Horse Ride competition.
  • It is the children’s responsibility to take participates in a Quiz Whizz competition, at the right time.
  • Time duration for a Quiz Whizz competition: Maximum 15 minutes.
  • It is the children’s responsibility to monitor and manage the time scheduled for completes a competition in time.
  • It’s the children’s responsibility to follow the 24-hour scheduled system [00:00 Hour – 23:59 Hour] to start and finish a competition in due time. For e.g. Competition Date: 11 th April, Expected Competition Duration: 00:15 minutes
    Child elects to start the competition at 11.05 on 11 th April. As it is a fifteen minutes program, the scheduled time for the competition therefore ends at 11:20.
    Competition submitted on or before 23:59 on 11th April Competition submitted on or after 00:00 on 12th April
    Within scheduled time Not possible to submit or No further access to the competition
    Treated as fair competition May result in withheld / refused
  • In an event that a child does not attempt the competition, due to illness or other adverse circumstances beyond their control, may submit a Good Cause claim in Exam Helpdesk within 3 working days of the game. If the Good Cause claim is accepted, the most common outcome is that the competition will need to be completed at a later date. * In some cases, some competition missed with Good Cause may be disregarded.
  • The children who experiences technical difficulties during a competition with accessing, completing, and/or submitting; should immediately contact to the Exam Helpdesk within 3 working days of the competition, using the contact details provided herewith and in the Application’s Contact Info.
    Exam Helpdesk:
    6371469050. [10:00 – 17:00] Monday – Friday [excluding national holidays]
  • Points are given for every correct answer in a competition.
  • There is no negative marking for wrong answers (No points are reduced for wrong answers).
  • There’s no option of review or change the answer, once progressed for the next question during a competition.
  • Skipping or returning from a game in between before completion of the competition, might result into withholding and also unable to attain the same competition later again.
  • Secured points: Total points achieved by the children for every correct answer in a competition.
  • Bonus Points: On the basis of secured points and percentage in a competition, the children are entitled for typically entry to a symbolic club and earned bonus points as follows:-

  • Terminal Points: The final output of a competition = Secured Points + Bonus Points For Example:- Turtle drive competition: A child scored 57 points out of 100 (57%) in a turtle drive competition, and entitled for entering the ENTHUSIAST club. The terminal points of the competition will be; 57 (secured points) + 66 (bonus points) = 123 Points. Horse ride competition: A child scored 95 points out of 100 (95%) in a horse ride competition, and entitled for entering the GENIUS club. The terminal points of the competition will be; 95 (secured points) + 444 (bonus points) = 539 points.
  • Monthly performance record: Will be defines on the basis of overall terminal points achieved by the children in a month.
  • Yearly performance record: Will be defines on the basis of overall terminal points achieved by the children in a year (12 consecutive months).
  • When two or more children obtain the same points in a competition, then their rank will be decided on the basis their consumption of time to finish the competition. For e.g. – XXX, YYY, ZZZ (3 children’s name) has secured 204 terminal points each, in a competition.
    But,
    XXX has finished the competition in 00:13:27 hour (13 minutes and 27 seconds). YYY has finished the competition in 00:14:05 hour (14 minutes and 05 seconds). ZZZ has finished the competition in 00:13:12 hour (13 minutes and 12 seconds).
    Here the ranks will be decided as: 1st – ZZZ (00:13:12), 2nd – XXX (00:13:27), and 3rd – YYY (00:14:05)
